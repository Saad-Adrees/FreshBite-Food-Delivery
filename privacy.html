Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Standard Account Data: We collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and payment details when you place an order or create an account with FreshBite.
  • VIP & Premium Member Data: For VIP members, we additionally store dietary preferences, favorite order presets, priority delivery instructions, and personalized support logs to deliver tailored dining experiences.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Order Fulfillment: To prepare, track, and deliver your food efficiently.
  • VIP Priority Routing: To identify VIP orders in our system for expedited kitchen preparation and priority courier assignment.
  • Communication: To send real-time order updates, promotional offers, and customer service follow-ups.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and secure servers to protect your personal information. Payment transactions are processed through encrypted payment gateways; we never store raw credit card numbers on our local servers.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share necessary delivery details (name, address, phone number) with our restaurant partners and delivery drivers solely for completing your order.

5. Your Rights

You may request to view, update, or delete your account data at any time by contacting us at hello@freshbite.com.
